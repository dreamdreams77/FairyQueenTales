A New Chapter of Fairyland
Chapter One

Something on the Stone Path

One morning, Princess Lauren was walking the stone path that wound from the meadow to the castle gate, the way she did most mornings, humming something with no particular tune. And there, right in the middle of the path, half hidden under a fallen leaf, sat a small purple calculator.

It was an unusual thing to find in fairyland, where most magic did not need buttons at all. Princess Lauren picked it up carefully, turned it over in her hands, and pressed one button just to see. Little numbers lit up, orderly and patient, waiting for someone to ask them something.

She did not know whose it was. But it was clearly loved, worn soft at the corners the way things get when they are held often, and she tucked it against her chest and decided she would find its owner before the sun was much higher.

Chapter Two

Gone Forever, LittleLove Was Sure

Meanwhile, behind the old oak nearest the castle wall, LittleLove was having a very hard morning. His calculator, purple and small and precisely his, was gone, and he had looked everywhere he could reach without stepping out from behind the tree, which was not very far at all.

He was certain it was gone forever. That was simply how these things went, he thought. Small things got lost on stone paths and were never seen again, and he curled in a little tighter behind the oak and glowed a dimmer, sadder glow than usual.

🔮 What LittleLove Was Thinking

Gone. Just gone. The way small purple things go when nobody is watching closely enough.

Chapter Three

He Isn't Shy, In Fact He's Lots of Fun

Princess Lauren found Fairy by the pond and held up the little purple calculator, still warm from her hands. "I found this on the stone path," she said. "I don't know whose it is. Could you take it to them? You know everyone in fairyland."

Fairy looked at it, and something in his face went soft and knowing all at once. "I know exactly whose this is," he said. "It's LittleLove's. He's probably worried sick behind that oak of his right now."

He handed it right back to her, gently, and shook his head when she tried to give it to him again. "Come with me," he said, "and give it to him yourself. He isn't shy. In fact, he's lots of fun, once somebody actually walks over and says hello."

Princess Lauren had always thought of LittleLove as the shy one behind the tree, the one who watched but never came close. She had never once thought to walk over and say hello herself.

Chapter Four

Handing It Over

So Princess Lauren walked with Fairy to the old oak, and for the first time she walked all the way around it instead of past it.

LittleLove blinked up at her, surprised, his glow brightening just slightly at the sight of the calculator in her hands. "You found it," he said, quiet and disbelieving. "I thought it was gone forever."

"It was right there on the stone path," Princess Lauren said, kneeling down so she was not towering over him. "I almost stepped right past it." She held it out, and he took it with both hands, pressing it briefly to his chest the way she had, before he even realized they had done the exact same thing without meaning to.

"Thank you," he said, and then, because he was, in fact, lots of fun once somebody actually stayed to talk, he added, "do you want to see what it does?"

Chapter Five

Hexadecimals, Fractions, and Just Drawing

And that was how it started. Princess Lauren sat down right there under the oak, crown and all, and LittleLove showed her the little purple calculator button by button, delighted to finally have someone to show.

He tried to teach her hexadecimals first, numbers that counted all the way up to sixteen before starting over, and Princess Lauren wrinkled her nose at that one. He tried fractions next, and she got those a little better, mostly because he drew them as slices of pie in the dirt with a stick, which she said made much more sense than the numbers ever did on their own.

✏️ Princess Lauren's Rule

Numbers are confusing. Pictures of numbers are not. Draw it first, then worry about the numbers after.

"It's so much easier when you just draw it," she told him, and LittleLove, who had never once thought of it that way, decided she might be onto something.

Chapter Six

Behind the Tree, and Not Behind the Tree

And so it went, most afternoons after that. LittleLove still liked his alone time behind the tree, and nobody, least of all Princess Lauren, ever tried to talk him out of that. Some days he simply wanted to sit quietly with his purple calculator and glow at his own small, contented pace, and that was perfectly all right.

But on the other days, Princess Lauren would come find him, and he would come out from behind the oak on his own two feet, and they would sit together in the grass while he taught her hexadecimals, and fractions, and all the other things Princess Lauren swore up and down were very confusing, right up until he drew them out for her, at which point they suddenly were not confusing at all.

"It's easier to just draw it," she told him, every single time, as if she were the one who had thought of it first. And LittleLove, glowing steady and unbothered behind his tree or right beside her in the grass, never once minded letting her think so.
